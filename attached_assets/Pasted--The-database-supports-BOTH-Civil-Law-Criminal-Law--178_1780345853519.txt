Identify all required database tables and structure them professionally.

--------------------------------------------------

CREATE THESE TABLES

1. legal_domains
Purpose:
Stores top-level legal domains.

Example:
- Criminal
- Civil

--------------------------------------------------

2. case_categories
Purpose:
Stores broad legal issue categories.

Examples:
- FIR Issues
- Cybercrime
- Tenant Disputes
- Property Disputes

--------------------------------------------------

3. legal_scenarios
Purpose:
Stores real-world user legal situations.

Examples:
- Police refusing FIR
- Illegal detention
- Deposit not returned
- Salary not paid

--------------------------------------------------

4. keywords
Purpose:
Maps user keywords to legal scenarios.

Examples:
- FIR
- landlord
- salary
- cyber fraud

--------------------------------------------------

5. legal_rights
Purpose:
Stores simplified legal rights awareness.

Examples:
- FIR can be filed at any police station
- Must be produced before magistrate within 24 hours

--------------------------------------------------

6. required_actions
Purpose:
Stores practical next-step guidance.

Examples:
- Submit written complaint
- Preserve payment proof
- Approach Superintendent of Police

--------------------------------------------------

7. document_requirements
Purpose:
Stores required supporting documents/evidence.

Examples:
- Rental agreement
- Salary slips
- Complaint copy

--------------------------------------------------

8. legal_timelines
Purpose:
Stores important legal timelines and deadlines.

Examples:
- Produce accused within 24 hours
- Notice response timeline

--------------------------------------------------

9. guided_questions
Purpose:
Stores dynamic legal refinement questions.

Examples:
- Was written complaint submitted?
- Is there a rental agreement?
- Was acknowledgement received?

--------------------------------------------------

10. lawyer_recommendations
Purpose:
Suggest suitable lawyer specialization and seniority.

IMPORTANT:
DO NOT rank lawyers.

Only suggest:
- specialization
- seniority level
- recommendation reason

Examples:
- Senior Criminal Lawyer
- Junior Civil Lawyer

--------------------------------------------------

11. nyayid_profiles
Purpose:
Stores generated structured legal profiles.

Includes:
- user input
- detected category
- detected scenario
- generated summary
- complexity level
